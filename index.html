How to Buy BITEX (BIT)

Follow these steps to purchase BITEX tokens safely:

  1. Install MetaMask or Trust Wallet
  2. Connect to the Polygon PoS network
  3. Ensure you have some MATIC for gas fees
  4. Go to QuickSwap DEX: Swap MATIC → BITEX
  5. Enter the amount and confirm the swap
  6. Always verify the contract address before purchasing
