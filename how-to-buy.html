How to Buy & Sell BITEX (BIT)

Complete guide for trading BITEX on Polygon Network

🔹 Token Information

Network: Polygon (PoS)

Standard: ERC-20

Contract Address:

0x4296dD83e49d01BB801172d8e938FafE6dcF86A0

⚠️ Always verify the contract address before buying.

🟢 How to Buy BITEX

Step 1: Install MetaMask Wallet
Download from: metamask.io
Step 2: Add Polygon Network to MetaMask
Step 3: Buy MATIC and send it to your wallet
Step 4: Go to QuickSwap
Open QuickSwap
Step 5: Paste BITEX Contract Address and swap MATIC → BIT

🔴 How to Sell BITEX

Step 1: Connect MetaMask to QuickSwap
Step 2: Select BITEX token
Step 3: Swap BIT → MATIC
Step 4: Confirm transaction in MetaMask

⚠️ Important Notes

  • Always double-check the contract address.
  • Beware of fake tokens.
  • Never share your private key or seed phrase.
  • Make sure you have enough MATIC for gas fees.
